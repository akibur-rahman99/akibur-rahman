MedLink
Kotlin · Android · Firebase

An Android health app for Admin, Doctor, Nurse, Patient and Guardian roles. It books appointment and lab slots, runs a walk-in queue, tracks medicine adherence, and reads prescriptions with Gemini-powered OCR.

Read the case studyHide the case study
Goal

One app for the whole patient journey: find the right department, book a slot, get seen, and keep taking the medicine.

What I built
  • Symptom-to-department advisor
  • Atomic slot booking for appointments and lab tests, so two people can never take the same slot
  • Walk-in queue with Add to Queue and Today's Patients
  • Medicine Score: morning, afternoon and night doses with 7-day, 30-day and 1-year trend graphs
  • Guardian Invite, a read-only link for family members
  • Doctor chat with text and images, Favorite Doctors and Doctors You've Visited
  • Prescription OCR with Gemini 2.5 Flash through Firebase AI Logic
  • Review insights for staff
Stack

Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication, Firebase AI Logic

How it evolved

It began as MediConnect, a Doctor and Patient prescription and reminder app on PostgreSQL, Node.js and Flutter. I moved it to Kotlin and Firebase and grew it from two roles to five: Admin, Doctor, Nurse, Patient and Guardian.

Context

Software build complete. Written up as my EEE 4103 capstone proposal at AIUB.

Next

Nurse and technician lab queue display, direct lab result upload, cancel and reschedule for appointments and lab bookings, and per-test analytics for admins.

Journey by Bus
C++ · OpenGL / Graphics

A four-scene interactive graphics app. Buses and vehicles move through live wind and rain, and you steer the camera and objects with mouse and keyboard.

Read the case studyHide the case study
Goal

Turn a bus journey into an interactive scene with live weather and full camera control, using C++ and OpenGL.

What I built
  • Four scenes: urban road, hillside and river, rainy night, foggy bridge
  • Bus and vehicle movement
  • Real-time wind and rain
  • Mouse and keyboard controls for the camera and objects
Method

I drew shapes in GeoGebra first to get exact coordinates, then converted them into C++ GLUT code in the glBegin / glVertex style.

Stack

C++, OpenGL, GLUT, GeoGebra

Context

Group project of four for the Computer Graphics course at AIUB.
